Privacy and terms

Last updated 21 November 2026

JobFit AI is a portfolio project built by Zafar Seepye. It compares a job advert with a CV and gives feedback on the fit. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to the text you paste and the terms for using the site. It is written in good faith but has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Privacy

What you send

When you click Analyse fit, the job advert and the CV you pasted are sent to the JobFit AI server. A CV usually contains personal information. Before pasting, we recommend removing anything the analysis doesn't need, such as your ID number, home address, date of birth and phone number.

How it is used

The text is used only to produce your analysis. The server sends it securely to Anthropic, the company that provides the Claude AI model, which returns the result.

  • Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days by default, except where it must keep them longer, for example to enforce its usage policies or by law. Read Anthropic's retention policy.
  • Anthropic does not use API data to train its models by default. Read Anthropic's training policy.

If the AI analysis is unavailable, a simple keyword check runs on the JobFit AI server instead, and the text isn't sent to Anthropic.

What JobFit AI stores

  • Your text: not stored. There is no database and no account system.
  • Cookies, tracking and analytics: none.
  • Your IP address: held briefly in the server's memory to enforce the daily limit, and discarded within 24 hours or when the server restarts.
  • Technical logs: the server logs errors and usage counts (such as how many words were processed), never the contents of your documents. The hosting provider may keep standard access logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask what personal information is held about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Because JobFit AI doesn't store your documents, there is normally nothing held. For any privacy question, contact Zafar Seepye at zafarseepye@gmail.com.

Terms of use

  • It's guidance, not a guarantee. The analysis is generated by AI and can be wrong or incomplete. It isn't career, legal or financial advice, and it can't predict whether you'll get a job.
  • Only paste what you have the right to use. Use your own CV, or one you have permission to share.
  • Fair use. Each visitor can run 5 analyses per day, and the site has an overall daily limit. Don't try to get around the limits, automate requests or overload the service.
  • No misuse. Don't use the site to process unlawful, harmful or other people's private content.
  • Provided as is. This is a free portfolio project. It may change, be unavailable or be taken down at any time, and it comes with no warranty.
